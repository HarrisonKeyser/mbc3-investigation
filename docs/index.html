How the game decides what you get

Your goal decides the session type and position. Every programmed session follows this 4-session cycle. Suppleness only comes up under Lose weight, and Breath only under Peace and calm or An active and balanced life, so you need to change goal at least once (Profile menu → Modify the goal). Changing goal keeps your completed exercises.

The Relaxation level slider decides the level. Lowest = Newb, middle = Normal, highest = Pro. Position 2 gives Normal for the first half of the relaxation time, then Newb. Position 4 gives Pro for the first half, then Normal. Many exercises exist at only one level (the N / Nm / P marks on each row).

Telling sessions apart on screen. The title during relaxation reads Simple breathing for the intro, Assisted breathing in a Breath session, and Exercises in a Pilates or Suppleness session. Pilates and Suppleness look alike, so use the cycle above: under Lose weight, sessions 2 and 4 are Suppleness. Floor exercises are done kneeling, sitting or lying down.

The game only counts exercises from the main relaxation blocks. Warm-ups and loosening moves don't count. A session that hangs on "Saving…" loses everything it added, so un-tick anything from a session that didn't save.

Recommended setup

The game has one way to play: Session on the main menu plays the next session of your program (fitness first, then relaxation). A program is a goal plus either a ready-made program or a Free Program you set up yourself (Profile menu → Modify the program). Relaxation time is what limits you, and Free Program gives the most:

  1. Free Program, Peace and calm: 90-minute sessions, 7 a week, 25 weeks. About 67 minutes of relaxation per session, alternating Breath and Pilates. Move the Relaxation slider through all five positions over the sessions. This also gets Master of Breathing.
  2. Then a new Free Program, Lose weight: weight target as high as it goes, 7 sessions a week, then the shortest program length offered. About 20 minutes of relaxation per session, every other one Suppleness. Cover all three levels again. Exercises you already completed in the game still count.
  3. “Twist your chest and bend your knees” counts as Suppleness but only appears in Pilates floor sessions at Pro (slider position 4 or 5).
  4. Missed your Zen goal? Accept the coach's offer of another session for more of the same type.
  5. Boost Up (answer "I don't feel very good" before a session) adds 2–3 minutes: stressed or angry gives standing Breath, tired or muscles sore (middle answer) gives standing Suppleness. Works under any goal.

More than one session a day? Yes. The first session each day is the planned one (or the next one, brought forward). Further sessions that day are free sessions: they reuse a session type from your calendar and don't save when they finish. To save after them, go to Options → Volume and confirm the three screens, or the progress is only written at the end of your next planned session.

Your efforts will pay off unlocks when you finish the last session of a program. Quickest: a Free Program with 1 session a week for 2 weeks, playing the second session the next day.
